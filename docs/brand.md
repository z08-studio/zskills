# ZSkills identity

The ZSkills mark is a solid, eight-spoke rounded asterisk in deep blue. Four
equal capsule bars cross at 45-degree intervals, preserving a connected center
and eight distinct round ends.

## Source and usage

`public/brand/mark.svg` is the editable source and the shared asset for the
application header, workspace overview, and browser favicon. Its 128-unit view
box contains a centered, 112-unit silhouette made from 19-unit-wide capsules.
Keep the mark proportional, with its existing clear space, and do not add a
background square, gradients, outlines, or a separate center dot.

The header pairs the mark with the existing system-font ZSkills wordmark. The
mark is decorative next to text; the home link retains its accessible name.

## Interface color

The primary brand color is deep blue, `#173b8f`. The accent tokens in
`src/styles.css` define its interaction states, focus ring, and pale blue
selection surfaces. Neutral reading surfaces remain white and light gray;
source categories and connection, warning, and error states keep distinct
semantic colors.
